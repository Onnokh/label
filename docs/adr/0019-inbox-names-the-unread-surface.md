# Inbox Names the Unread Surface, Reading Queue Is Retired

Sleevy's ubiquitous language had two names for the iOS home surface, and they disagreed about what that surface holds. **Reading Queue** entered the glossary first, when the home list showed all **Saved Items** newest first, and CONTEXT.md still defined it that way: "the primary list of all Saved Items." The **Inbox** arrived later with the Unread-Only Inbox work, turned the **Home Tab** into an unread-only triage surface titled "Inbox", and its glossary entry already said "_Avoid_: Reading Queue." But the specs for the iOS retrieval layer (PLO-446, PLO-447) and the code they produced kept the old word for the new thing: `RetrievalRequest.readingQueue` projected unread-only items and fed the screen titled "Inbox". An agent or engineer reading CONTEXT.md would build a Reading Queue with every Saved Item in it; one reading the code would believe a Reading Queue is unread-only. One of the two had to win.

**Inbox wins.** The glossary had already decided this — the Inbox entry forbade "Reading Queue", the Home Tab entry named the screen "Inbox", and the Folder ambiguity note contrasted Folders with "unread triage like the Inbox" — and the product agrees: the user-visible screen title is "Inbox", and the surface's whole behavior (items leave when read, **All Caught Up** when empty, **Renewed Intent** returns items) is inbox semantics, not queue semantics. The Reading Queue as defined — a home list of all Saved Items — is a surface that no longer exists anywhere in the product; its browsing role belongs to the **Library**. So rather than redefine Reading Queue to mean the unread surface (which would put two names on one concept forever), the term is retired: the glossary entry and the **Queue Tab** entry are removed, every relationship that placed capture flows "at the top of the Reading Queue" now says Inbox (which is where an unread new capture in fact appears), and the iOS names follow — `RetrievalRequest.readingQueue` is now `RetrievalRequest.inbox` and `ReadingListStore.readingQueueSnapshot` is now `inboxSnapshot`. This is a rename only; no projection, ordering, or coverage behavior changed. The cost is that PLO-446 and PLO-447 still say "Reading Queue" for the unread surface in their historical text, and older marketing copy uses "reading queue" loosely; specs are read as history, and marketing prose is not bound by the ubiquitous language, so neither is rewritten.
